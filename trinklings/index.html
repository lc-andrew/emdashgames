How to play

  • Each turn you get 10 to spend in the shop. It doesn't carry over.
  • Trinklings and Gear come in six tiers. A new tier opens every second turn, and the higher the tier, the stronger the Trinkling. Tap a card to see its tier.
  • Every Trinkling shows two chips: a grey Attack and a red Health (X / Y). So "+2/+2" means +2 attack and +2 health.
  • Tap a shop Trinkling, then tap an empty squad slot to buy it (3 ).
  • Tap a squad Trinkling, then another slot to reorder — the rightmost one fights first ▶.
  • Some Trinklings move the line once the fight starts: they swap (⇄) — trade places with a friend — or shove an enemy to the front or the back of its line. Whoever ends up at the front is who swings next, so the order you set is not always the order you get.
  • Buy copies of the same Trinkling to combine them (bigger stats + it levels up), or drop two different ones together to fuse them (12 , or 15 straight from the shop). The new Trinkling is reborn at 1/1, but keeps both abilities at their levels and gains a unique ✦ Signature Move.
  • A fused Trinkling has several abilities — each fires on its own trigger (the tag beside it). Tap it to read them all. A 1/1 faints fast: give it gear, and fuse spare pets rather than your strongest.
  • Gear is what you bring. Pick up to six before a run; only those show up in the shop. Each costs 3 to give to a Trinkling. Tap a piece of Gear, then tap the Trinkling that gets it (or drag it across). Some is a permanent stat boost; some is held and acts in the fight — it blocks, lures, goes first, echoes. One held piece of each kind per Trinkling; fusing keeps it.
  • Tap a squad Trinkling and hit Sell to get some gold back.
  • Reroll the shop for new Trinklings, or select a shop card and hit Freeze to keep it next turn (free).
  • Hit End Turn to start a fight — the auto-battle. Boosts "for the fight" last one battle; (permanent) ones stay forever.
  • Win 10 fights to collect 10 and take the Cup!
  • Lose a fight, lose a . At 0 the game ends — no Trinkling is ever truly hurt.
