How to play

  • Each turn you get 10 to spend in the shop. It doesn't carry over.
  • Every Trinkling shows two chips: a grey Attack and a red Health (X / Y). So "+2/+2" means +2 attack and +2 health.
  • Tap a shop Trinkling, then tap an empty squad slot to buy it (3 ).
  • Tap a squad Trinkling, then another slot to reorder — the rightmost one fights first ▶.
  • Buy copies of the same Trinkling to combine them (bigger stats + it levels up), or drop two different ones together to fuse a brand-new Trinkling.
  • Items are bonuses you buy (2 ) and feed to a Trinkling for a permanent boost.
  • Tap a squad Trinkling and hit Sell to get some gold back.
  • Reroll the shop for new Trinklings, or select a shop item and hit Freeze to keep it next turn (free).
  • Hit End Turn to start a fight — the auto-battle. Boosts "for the fight" last one battle; (permanent) ones stay forever.
  • Win 10 fights to collect 10 and take the Cup!
  • Lose a fight, lose a . At 0 the game ends — no Trinkling is ever truly hurt.
