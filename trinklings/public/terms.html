Trinklings — Terms of sale

Last updated: 2026-09-19

These terms cover buying Dash Points in Trinklings, made by Emdash Games. Trinklings is free to play: you never have to buy anything, and every part of the game can be reached by playing. Buying Dash Points is optional and only ever saves you time.

What you are buying

  • Dash Points are a digital item inside the game. You are buying a licence to use them in Trinklings — not a physical good, and not money. They have no cash value: they cannot be exchanged for money, transferred between accounts, sold, or converted into fusion points.
  • Dash Points buy cosmetics only — banners, emotes and arena skins. They never affect a battle, your rating, your rank or who you are matched against.
  • Some items can never be bought. Rank rewards are earned by climbing the ladder, and some Shop items are marked Play only: fusion points earn them, Dash Points cannot.
  • Dash Points and what you buy with them are tied to the account that bought them. If you delete your account they are gone, and they are not restored if you sign up again.

Price, payment and delivery

  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. The price you pay is the one shown on Stripe's checkout page before you confirm.
  • Dash Points are added to your account as soon as Stripe confirms the payment — usually within seconds. If they have not appeared after a few minutes, contact us (below) with the date and amount.

Refunds

  • Because Dash Points are delivered immediately, we do not offer automatic refunds for a change of mind.
  • If something went wrong — you were charged twice, the Dash Points never arrived, or a purchase was made on your account without your permission — write to us through the in-game feedback form (Settings ▸ Send feedback) and we will put it right.
  • Your consumer-law rights are not affected by anything on this page, including your rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act and Fair Trading Act, and any equivalent rights where you live.

Who can buy

  • You must be 13 or older to have a Trinklings account. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian before buying anything, and must use their payment method with their consent.
  • Parents: if a purchase was made on your card without your permission, tell us through the feedback form and we will refund it.

Changes and availability

  • We may change prices, add items, or stop selling a pack; Dash Points you have already bought stay in your account. If Trinklings ever shuts down we will say so in the game beforehand and stop selling Dash Points at that point.

Contact

Emdash Games — use the in-game feedback form (Settings ▸ Send feedback). See also our Privacy page.
